About College

SMT S.R. Patel Engineering College, Unjha is a reputed engineering institution committed to providing quality technical education. The college offers modern infrastructure, experienced faculty, well-equipped laboratories, and a learning environment that promotes innovation, research, and overall student development.

Courses

Computer Engineering

Focuses on programming, software development, AI, networking, cybersecurity, and data science.

Civil Engineering

Deals with construction, structural design, transportation, and environmental engineering.

Electrical Engineering

Covers power systems, electrical machines, electronics, and renewable energy.

Electronics & Communication

Focuses on communication systems, embedded systems, VLSI, and IoT.

Campus Life and Student Development

A vibrant campus environment promotes academic excellence, leadership, and personal growth. Students participate in technical workshops, coding competitions, cultural festivals, sports events, seminars, and community service activities. These experiences help build confidence, teamwork, communication skills, and a strong professional mindset.
